# Missing space

Checks for spaces around operators (=, <, >, <=, >=, <>, %, +, -, *, /) and after comma and semicolon. Comments and string literals are not analyzed. Arithmetic signs are checked on the left side only (as in BSL LS by default). The check has no quick fix.

- [BSL Language Server: MissingSpace](https://1c-syntax.github.io/bsl-language-server/diagnostics/MissingSpace/)
